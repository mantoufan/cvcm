## Short (EN)

Browser-local PDF, image, QR, and clipboard tools. Files never leave your device. No signup.

## Long (EN)

cv.cm is a free, open-source browser toolkit. Merge, split, and compress PDFs; convert HEIC, WebP, and JPG; generate QR codes and barcodes; watermark, crop, and strip EXIF from photos. Image and PDF tools run in the tab — files are not uploaded. A short-lived cloud clipboard shares text or files with an ultra-short link (gone after 10 views or 24 hours). No account required.
